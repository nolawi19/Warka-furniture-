How it is made

Kebena, Addis Ababa

Dried a year

Air-dried before anything is cut, so joints stay tight through the dry season.

Cut to one joint

Every mortise is marked to its own tenon. Parts are not interchangeable, on purpose.

Oiled, not lacquered

Four coats of hardwax oil. Scratches spot-repair instead of needing a full refinish.

Four weeks

From order to your floor, delivered and levelled anywhere in Addis.

Five parts

Laid out left to right in the order they are cut. Same scale, no screws between them.
